# Kestrel Uptime, Inc. (fictional): Overview
> Last reviewed: 2026-09-15 · Owner: VP Strategy · FICTIONAL TEACHING COMPANY

- **What we sell:** Predictive-maintenance SaaS plus a retrofit vibration and temperature sensor kit that warns plant teams days before critical equipment fails.
- **HQ:** Columbus, Ohio, USA. Founded 2016. About 210 employees, all in the US.
- **Scale (fictional):** about 140 customer plants, ARR about USD 38M, growing 30% a year.
- **Mission:** No unplanned downtime in mid-size manufacturing.
- **Vision:** The default maintenance co-pilot for mid-size plants in North America and Europe by 2030.

## Strategic priorities, next 24 months
1. Open a first EU market and reach first EU revenue within 12 months of the decision.
2. Keep net revenue retention above 115% in the US.
3. Launch an OEM partner channel (equipment makers embedding Kestrel).
