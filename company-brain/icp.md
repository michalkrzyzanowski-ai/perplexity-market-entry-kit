# ICP and Personas
> Last reviewed: 2026-09-15 · FICTIONAL

## Primary segment
- **Industry:** discrete manufacturing: automotive parts, metal fabrication, plastics and packaging, household appliances.
- **Size:** single plants of 150 to 1,000 employees; groups with up to 10 plants.
- **Situation:** rotating equipment (motors, pumps, compressors, conveyors) is critical; maintenance is still calendar-based or reactive; small reliability team (0 to 3 engineers).
- **Triggers:** a costly unplanned stoppage, a new plant manager, a customer OEM demanding delivery reliability, labour shortage in maintenance.

## Personas
| Persona | Cares about | Words they use |
|---|---|---|
| Plant manager (economic buyer) | OEE, delivery penalties, overtime | "We lost two shifts when the compressor died" |
| Maintenance lead (champion) | Fewer night call-outs, spare-parts planning | "I need to know which motor goes next" |
| IT/OT manager (gatekeeper) | Network security, data leaving the plant | "Nothing connects to our PLCs without review" |
| Group CFO (approver, multi-plant) | Payback under 12 months | "Show me the downtime cost first" |

## Anti-ICP
- Process industries needing hazardous-area certified sensors (chemicals, oil and gas).
- Plants under 100 employees (cannot absorb onboarding).
- Enterprises requiring on-premise deployment only.
