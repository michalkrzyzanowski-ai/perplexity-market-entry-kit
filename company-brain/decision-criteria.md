# Decision Criteria and Risk Appetite
> Last reviewed: 2026-09-15 · FICTIONAL · Owner: CEO

## Risk appetite
Low to moderate. The board prefers staged commitments with a clear stop point over a large launch.

## Entry is supported when
- Enough ICP plants in the target market to reach the 3-year EU ARR goal (evidence required, not assumed).
- A credible route to market that does not require more than 4 hires in year one.
- Regulatory and certification work can be completed within 9 months and inside the budget.
- Existing US customers with local plants can serve as reference sites.

## Entry is ruled out when
- EU data hosting is mandatory for the segment and cannot be delivered within 9 months.
- Sensor certification would take longer than 12 months.
- Local-language product and support are required from day one for most buyers.
- Payback for the EU investment is longer than 36 months in the base case.
