# Offer and Positioning
> Last reviewed: 2026-09-15 · FICTIONAL

## Offer
- **Kestrel Platform:** anomaly detection and remaining-useful-life estimates on vibration and temperature data, work-order suggestions, integrations with common CMMS tools.
- **Kestrel Sense kit:** wireless retrofit sensors and gateway, installed in under one day per line.
- **Pricing model:** per monitored asset per year, plus hardware at cost. Typical first-year plant contract USD 60k to 150k.
- **Onboarding:** 8-week remote-first rollout with one on-site install visit.

## Positioning
For mid-size plants that cannot staff a reliability engineering team, Kestrel is the predictive-maintenance service that pays back within a year, unlike enterprise asset-performance suites that need months of integration and a data science team.

## Differentiators (with proof)
- Install in a day: median 6 hours per line across the last 50 installs.
- Payback: customer-reported median payback 9 months (internal survey, 41 respondents).
- Built for small teams: plain-language alerts, no dashboards to interpret.

## Competitors and alternatives
- Enterprise asset-performance suites from large industrial software vendors.
- Sensor-maker platforms bundled with hardware.
- Local maintenance service firms doing periodic manual vibration analysis.
- Doing nothing (run to failure).
