# Assumptions and unknowns

Status: working draft. This file is not a human approval record.

## Classification guide

- `[COMPANY FACT]`: stated in a supplied company file
- `[PARTICIPANT INPUT]`: supplied during an interview; not independently checked
- `[EXTERNAL EVIDENCE]`: supported by a cited external source
- `[GOAL]`: desired outcome, not a demonstrated fact
- `[ASSUMPTION]`: working premise requiring validation
- `[UNKNOWN]`: missing information; do not infer
- `[AI INTERPRETATION]`: synthesis based on stated inputs
- `[RECOMMENDATION]`: proposed action, subject to human approval

## Rules

- Do not convert an unknown into an assumption without labeling it.
- Do not convert a goal into a forecast.
- Do not use an assumption as evidence of demand, cost, compliance, or payback.
- Record any ambiguity or conflict in the source material.
