# Executive deck outline

Status: AI-assisted draft; human review pending.

## Design principles

- One conclusion-led headline per slide.
- One primary message and visual per slide.
- Keep detailed sources and limitations in notes or an appendix.
- Do not imply that estimates, assumptions, or AI findings are verified facts.

## Appendix checklist

- Evidence ledger
- Exact source URLs
- Publication dates and observation periods
- Geography/population definitions
- Assumptions and unknowns
- Review findings
- Human-verification status
