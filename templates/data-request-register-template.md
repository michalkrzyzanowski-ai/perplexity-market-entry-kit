## Prioritization rule

Prioritize data requests that could change the option ranking, invalidate a preferred option, or prevent an irreversible commitment.

## Notes

- Unknown cost is not zero.
- Customer interest is not a signed commitment.
- A general market statistic is not automatically evidence of ICP demand.
- A generic regulatory summary is not a configuration-specific legal conclusion.
