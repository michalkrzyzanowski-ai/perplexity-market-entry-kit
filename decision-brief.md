# Kestrel Uptime: market-entry decision brief

FICTIONAL TEACHING SCENARIO. All internal company figures are invented.

| Field | Scenario |
|---|---|
| Decision | Which first EU market, Poland or Germany, should Kestrel validate next, and how? Postponement is a legitimate outcome. |
| Owner | Fictional CEO, with board approval |
| Audience | Executive team |
| Offer | Predictive-maintenance platform plus retrofit sensor kit |
| ICP | Discrete manufacturing plants with 150–1,000 employees; see icp.md |
| Horizon | Decision in Q4 2026; first EU revenue within 12 months if entry is justified |
| Goal, not forecast | USD 3M EU ARR within three years |
| Constraints | USD 2.5M over 18 months, at most four EU hires in year one, English-only product, US-hours support; see capabilities-and-constraints.md |
| Options | Poland customer-led pilot; Germany customer-led pilot; postpone and resolve readiness gaps. Consider partner support within either pilot. |
| Excluded | Other countries, acquisitions, process industries, a full legal opinion |
| Evidence window | Prefer latest applicable evidence. Explain the observation period as well as publication date; justify older sources. |
| Output | One-page executive summary, short market/fit comparison, 3–5 decision-critical claims, options, and unresolved questions. Use market-entry-report.md. |

## Questions to investigate

1. Where is there evidence of demand from the actual target segment, not manufacturing generally?
2. What route to buyers and installation/support is feasible within the company constraints?
3. Which commercial, language, hosting, or sensor-conformity questions could prevent a pilot?

Separate legal requirements, buyer preferences, and assumptions. If applicability is uncertain, state what a qualified reviewer must resolve. Do not infer that EU hosting is universally mandatory.

## Missing information is part of the exercise

Do not assume customer interest is a signed commitment. Actual EU willingness to pay, margins, recurring revenue per plant, certification scope/timing, hosting costs, and partner terms are not established. Do not calculate investment payback without these inputs. Ask up to five questions, then explicitly record unanswered items.

The first-year contract range includes hardware; it is not recurring ARR. Do not use it as an ARR-per-plant estimate. Existing-company averages need not describe new EU pilot economics.
