# Human quality checklist

A link is not proof. "Verified" means checking that the source supports the exact claim, not merely opening the page.

## Before research

- [ ] Decision, options, offer, segment, horizon, and owner are explicit.
- [ ] The agent can read the supplied files and cites them for company information.
- [ ] Fictional facts, goals, assumptions, and unknowns are separate.
- [ ] Missing information is answered or explicitly left unresolved.
- [ ] A human reviewed and approved the bounded plan.

## Before relying on the report

- [ ] Each decision-critical external claim has a usable URL and relevant source details.
- [ ] A human checked the original source against the claim, including units, geography, population, and observation period.
- [ ] Unavailable sources and unchecked claims remain labelled; AI review is not human verification.
- [ ] Market attractiveness is assessed separately from company fit.
- [ ] Assumptions, forecasts, and internal goals are not presented as external facts.
- [ ] Contradictions and counter-evidence are visible.
- [ ] A pilot in either market and postponement were considered.
- [ ] Legal requirements and buyer preferences are distinguished; unresolved legal applicability goes to qualified review.
- [ ] Costs, ARR, and payback are not calculated from incompatible or missing inputs.
- [ ] The recommendation states its conditions and what could reverse it.
- [ ] The decision owner knows what remains unverified and approves only the appropriate next step.
