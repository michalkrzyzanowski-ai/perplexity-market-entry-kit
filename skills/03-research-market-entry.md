# Skill 03 — Research market entry

## Purpose

Conduct only the approved research plan and produce an AI-assisted decision draft.

## Required inputs

- Explicit human approval of `decision/research-plan.md`
- Company-context files or `decision/company-context-summary.md`
- Completed decision brief
- `decision/research-plan.md`
- Applicable report template

## Research rules

1. Use company files only for company context and cite the filename for every company fact.
2. Treat company goals as goals, not evidence or forecasts.
3. Prefer primary or official external sources.
4. Record for every material external claim:
   - Exact claim
   - URL
   - Source title
   - Publisher
   - Publication date
   - Observation period
   - Geography and population
   - Relevant limitation
5. Distinguish publication date from observation period. Do not reject an older source automatically; explain whether it is still applicable.
6. Search for counter-evidence.
7. Separate legal requirements, buyer preferences, company facts, assumptions, and unknown applicability questions.
8. Never infer unknown market size, demand, willingness to pay, legal applicability, cost, margin, payback, or ROI.
9. Keep market attractiveness separate from company fit.
10. Compare all stated options, including postponement/no-go.

## Required outputs

Create:

- `decision/evidence-ledger.md`
- `decision/market-entry-draft.md`

The draft must include:

- One-page maximum executive summary
- 3–5 decision-critical claims
- Attractiveness-versus-fit comparison
- Options and conditional recommendation or insufficient-evidence conclusion
- Counter-evidence and unresolved questions
- Next validation actions with proposed owners
- Human review section with all unverified claims marked `Not yet checked`

## Do not

- Do not claim human verification.
- Do not issue a final human decision.
- Do not force a country, vendor, market, or investment selection when key evidence is missing.

## Human gate

The draft is ready for review, not approval. Ask for a review instruction or reviewer findings.
