# Skill 01 — Build or validate company context

## Purpose

Create a usable, decision-specific context pack before external research.

## Required inputs

- `decision/workflow-state.md`, if it exists
- File-based or hybrid mode: available company files and the completed decision brief
- Interview-based mode: participant answers
- `templates/assumptions-and-unknowns-template.md`
- `templates/data-request-register-template.md`

## File-based mode

1. List every required file.
2. Confirm readability with one specific detail from each file; do not infer access from filenames.
3. Cite each company fact by filename.
4. Identify missing, blank, unreadable, stale, conflicting, or ambiguous inputs.
5. Restate the decision, scope, options, horizon, constraints, and decision owner.

## Interview-based mode

Ask no more than five questions in one turn. Start with:

1. What decision must be made, including realistic options and postponement/no-go?
2. Who owns the decision, and when is it needed?
3. What offer, initiative, or investment is being evaluated?
4. Which customers, geographies, or business units are in scope or excluded?
5. What constraints or conditions could rule an option out?

After answers, ask only the next questions needed to establish ICP, evidence already available, capacity, risks, and decision criteria. Label every answer `[PARTICIPANT INPUT]` unless it is later supported by a company file.

## Hybrid mode

Use readable files first. Ask only questions whose answers are not established in those files.

## Required outputs

Create or update:

- `decision/company-context-summary.md`
- `decision/context-and-gaps.md`
- `decision/assumptions-and-unknowns.md`
- `decision/data-request-register.md`

## Required classifications

Use these labels consistently:

- `[COMPANY FACT]`
- `[PARTICIPANT INPUT]`
- `[EXTERNAL EVIDENCE]`
- `[GOAL]`
- `[ASSUMPTION]`
- `[UNKNOWN]`
- `[AI INTERPRETATION]`
- `[RECOMMENDATION]`

## Do not

- Do not conduct external research.
- Do not calculate market size, demand, cost, payback, or ROI without inputs.
- Do not label a participant answer or AI finding human-verified.
- Do not recommend an option.

## Human gate

Ask the participant to confirm that the context pack is sufficient to plan research. If it is not, continue the interview or record unresolved gaps.
