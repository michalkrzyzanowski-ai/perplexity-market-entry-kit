# Skill 02 — Plan research

## Purpose

Propose a bounded, decision-specific research plan. A plan is not authorization to conduct research.

## Required inputs

- `decision/company-context-summary.md`
- `decision/context-and-gaps.md`
- `decision/assumptions-and-unknowns.md`
- `decision/data-request-register.md`
- Completed decision brief
- Applicable report template

## Plan structure

Use three workstreams. Adapt the names to the decision if necessary.

### 1. Demand and target-customer evidence

State:

- The demand questions to answer for the actual ICP
- Preferred primary and official sources
- Counter-evidence to search for
- What would count as insufficient evidence

### 2. Route to market and company fit

State:

- Buyer access, sales route, partner, installation, service, support, localization, and capacity questions
- Company facts to test against external evidence
- What would count as insufficient evidence

### 3. Readiness barriers and unresolved dependencies

State:

- Product, security, data, hosting, regulatory, procurement, contractual, and operating-model questions
- Which issues require qualified specialist review
- What would count as insufficient evidence

## Required plan boundaries

- Define decision options, scope, geography, target customer, exclusions, time horizon, and evidence window.
- Limit the planned final output to 3–5 material claims.
- Specify that each material external claim needs a source URL, title, publisher, publication date, observation period, geography/population, and limitations.
- Separate company facts, external evidence, assumptions, interpretation, recommendations, and open questions.
- Include a strategy for evidence against any emerging preferred option.
- State the conditions that would result in a conditional recommendation or insufficient-evidence finding.

## Output

Create `decision/research-plan.md`.

## Do not

- Do not search the web.
- Do not draft the final recommendation.
- Do not presume that an external source will establish a required fact.

## Human gate

Ask for explicit approval of `decision/research-plan.md` before beginning research.
